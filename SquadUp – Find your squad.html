How it works

1
Pick your game

Tap a game card to find local profiles that chose it, or filter squad requests by game, rank, region, or mic.

2
Join or post

Try posting a session time, saving a request, joining a squad, or managing your own request.

3
Try the chat

Use the local squad room to test the flow. It is not a live chat with players on other devices.

Play fair

Be respectful. No hate, harassment, cheating services, account selling or sharing personal details. Never send money to a stranger. Close your request when your squad is full.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes, posting, joining and chatting are free.

Which games and platforms?

21 game options across shooters, battle royale, sports, MOBA, fighting and sandbox titles, on PlayStation, Xbox, PC and Mobile. Pick Other game for anything not listed.

Is this connected to other players?

No. This demo keeps accounts, requests, profiles, and chat in this browser. Other people and devices cannot see or join your data.

How do I get into the game with your squad?

This demo does not contact game services. You can use its local room chat to try the flow, then share an in-game ID or party code yourself when you play.

How do I leave or close a request?

Press Leave on a squad you joined, or Close on a request you posted.
